SECTION 01 // OVERVIEW

Project Overview & Scientific Challenge

QuantumShield is a quantum-powered antibiotic discovery platform designed to engineer novel drug candidates against drug-resistant pathogens (Mycobacterium tuberculosis, SARS-CoV-2, Salmonella).

Chemical Space limit: With 10^60 potential drug-like molecules, classical computers hit exponential bottlenecks simulating entangled electrons. Mutated target sites (like I194T in TB's InhA enzyme) render standard antibiotics ineffective.

Quantum Solution: Maps mutated active pocket orbitals directly onto qubits to solve exact binding energy (ΔG) without classical approximations.

SECTION 02 // TECH STACK

Drug Discovery Technology Stack

  • 1. NIM Target Extraction (NVIDIA): meta/llama-3.1-8b extracts target protein, UniProt ID, and pocket specifications.
  • 2. Protein Structures (AlphaFold DB): Downloads 3D .pdb structures, parsing and centering active pocket atoms.
  • 3. Molecular Evolution (RDKit): Genetic Algorithm evolves scaffolds, minimizing interatomic energy via MMFF94.
  • 4. Quantum Chemistry (IBM Qiskit): Maps orbital Hamiltonian and runs VQE to find electronic ground-state energies.
SECTION 03 // SIMULATION PHYSICS

Mathematical & Physics Engine

Thermodynamic Binding Energy (ΔG):

ΔE_binding = E_complex - (E_protein + E_drug)

Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE):

E(θ) = ⟨ψ(θ)|H|ψ(θ)⟩ / ⟨ψ(θ)|ψ(θ)⟩

Qubit mapping supports Jordan-Wigner, Bravyi-Kitaev, and Parity. We estimate clinical dissociation values (Kd) and blockade rates:

Kd = 10^(ΔG / 1.36) M // Blockade = C / (C + Kd)

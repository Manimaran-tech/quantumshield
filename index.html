03 / QUANTUM RL POLICY

Quantum RL Agent (QRL)

Executes a parameterized quantum circuit (PQC) acting as a policy model. Superconducting qubits apply variational RY and RZ gate operations, learning to select chemical modification actions (such as bioisostere substitutions, scaffold hopping, or ring expansion) to optimize candidate molecules.

04 / QUANTUM FEEDBACK

Biophysical Quantum Rewards

Guides the QRL agent using a hybrid reward function combining quantum chemical ground-state energy calculations, MMFF94 force field constraints, pocket steric fit values, and drug-likeness rules (QED and Lipinski) to steer molecular mutations toward optimal bindings.

05 / QUANTUM CHEMISTRY

Solves molecular ground-state electronic energies by mapping active valence orbitals onto qubit Hamiltonians. Supports Jordan-Wigner and Bravyi-Kitaev mappings to perform chemical energy minimizations and calculate accurate binding affinities.

06 / HARDWARE CO-DESIGN

QPU Topology Co-Design

Optimizes PQC gate mappings according to physical QPU coupling topologies. Minimizes SWAP gate overhead and applies Zero-Noise Extrapolation error mitigation to ensure highly accurate results on real superconducting quantum hardware.
